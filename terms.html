Terms of Service

Last updated: May 23, 2026

LifeLink connects donors, hospitals, blood banks, patients, NGOs, volunteers and administrators for blood donation coordination. The platform supports coordination, but it is not a replacement for licensed medical judgment or emergency services.

Healthcare Responsibility

Hospitals, blood banks and medical professionals are responsible for clinical decisions, blood safety checks, donor eligibility verification, transfusion protocols and legal compliance in their region.

User Responsibilities

Users must provide accurate information, use the platform lawfully, avoid false emergency requests, protect account credentials and respect donor and patient privacy.

Donor And Patient Safety

Donors should provide truthful health and donation history. Patients and hospitals should confirm blood compatibility, availability and medical requirements through authorized healthcare staff.

Payments And Subscriptions

Paid plans may be processed through Cashfree or Razorpay. A subscription should be treated as active only after successful backend verification and payment provider confirmation.

Availability

LifeLink may be unavailable during maintenance, infrastructure incidents, database outages, payment provider downtime, SMS or email provider downtime, or map provider outages.
