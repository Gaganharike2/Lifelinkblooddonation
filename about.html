About LifeLink

LifeLink

LifeLink is a blood donation management platform built to connect donors, hospitals, blood banks, patients, NGOs and volunteers through faster discovery, verified profiles, emergency alerts and real-time coordination.

Join LifeLink Contact us
Verified Network

Role-based access for donors, hospitals, blood banks, NGOs, patients and admins keeps workflows organized and accountable.

Location Aware

City and map workflows help teams find nearby donors, hospitals and blood support quickly.

Emergency Ready

Blood requests, donor alerts, notifications and dashboards are designed for urgent healthcare coordination.

Our mission

LifeLink aims to reduce the time between blood need and donor availability by giving hospitals, patients and community organizations one connected platform for requests, inventory, communication and donor engagement.
