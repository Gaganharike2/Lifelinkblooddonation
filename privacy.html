Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

LifeLink processes personal, contact, health, location, payment and communication data to operate blood donation, emergency request, hospital, donor, NGO, patient and support workflows.

Data We Collect

We may collect account details, role information, blood group, donor health profile, hospital or organization details, patient request information, appointment records, city and map location, uploaded documents, support messages, payment metadata, notifications and audit logs.

How We Use Data

We use data for account verification, donor matching, emergency alerts, request tracking, hospital and blood bank coordination, payment processing, fraud prevention, support, security monitoring and platform analytics.

Location Data

LifeLink uses location data to show nearby donors, hospitals and blood banks. If exact latitude and longitude are not available, city-level map coordinates may be used for search and display.

Payments

Subscription payments may be processed through Cashfree and Razorpay. LifeLink stores payment status and order metadata, but payment card or bank details are handled by the payment provider.

Security

Production deployments should use HTTPS, strong authentication secrets, access controls, payment signature verification, restricted API keys, database backups and monitoring.

User Rights

Users may request access, correction, deletion, grievance review or withdrawal of consent where applicable. Identity verification may be required before processing a request.
